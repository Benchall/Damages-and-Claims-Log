Getting these figures into wages and the P&L

Driver Pay Log: “Download for Driver Pay Log” gives one line per claim deducted this pay period, dated the last day of the period. Fines and PCNs come through as Parking ticket; van, parcel and third-party claims as Van damage (the note says which). Refunds after a won dispute come through as Top up. Driver names here must match the pay log.

P&L Tracker: “Download for P&L” gives the cost the business absorbs on every claim whose incident date falls in this pay period, by FD, email and depot. Put it in the Van damages box on that FD's daily entry.

Paused lines (an open dispute, or no signed agreement if you've chosen to hold those) are left out of the download and the claim's remaining instalments move on to later pay periods.

FDs

Each FD is linked to an email address and a depot, as in the P&L Tracker. Every claim is logged against an FD, so you can see which FD, email and depot a charge belongs to.

Drivers

Use the same name as in the Driver Pay Log, so the deductions line up. Their usual FD fills in when you log a claim, and can be changed per claim.
